# Nemlig Assistant 6.2.3 - Linden

## In plain language

The Draft list now gives clearer, quieter entry, confirmation, and outcome screens without changing what Nemlig Assistant is allowed to add.

## Changed

- Moved the live viewer resource to v15 and retired v14 as a read-only page.
- Made an empty or ended local Draft list a dedicated conversational starting surface without destination tabs or destructive local actions.
- Simplified the prepared confirmation, verified-success, and uncertain-submission presentations while retaining exact review, explicit confirmation, verification, and no automatic retry.
- Added a compact local-actions disclosure for ending an active Draft list.
