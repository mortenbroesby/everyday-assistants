# Nemlig Assistant 6.2.0 - Rowan

## In plain language

To decide product rows now keep their decision controls out of the way until
you open the product, while Ready keeps quantity controls immediately visible.
The Draft-list viewer now reuses small shared display components so later visual
work stays consistent without changing basket behavior.

## Changed

- Added viewer-local product summary, quantity, facts, tabs, and action-footer
  components.
- Kept local Draft-list state, protected submission, and the stable viewer URI
  unchanged.
