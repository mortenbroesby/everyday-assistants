# Nemlig Assistant 6.2.6 - Meadow

## In plain language

Large Draft lists now have a clear next step when every product is Ready: open
Ready to make the final check. The local list supports up to 500 products,
while all real Nemlig basket safeguards remain unchanged.

## Changed

- Replaced the legacy 50-product Draft-list limit with one 500-product
  contract across the MCP schemas and review service.
- Added a deliberate completion state when every product has moved to Ready,
  including a local-only route to the Ready tab.
- Added viewer smoke and visual-contract coverage for the completion state and
  service coverage for the Draft-list boundary.

## Safety

- No provider basket is changed when navigating from the completion state.
- The protected add-only real-basket authorization, revalidation, readback,
  and uncertain-write handling paths are unchanged.
