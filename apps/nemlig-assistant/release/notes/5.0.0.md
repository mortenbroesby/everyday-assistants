# Nemlig Assistant 5.0.0 - Unhurried

## In plain language

Nemlig Assistant no longer stops household shopping bursts because of its own per-minute request limits. Daily and monthly cost ceilings, authentication and exact basket approval remain. Nemlig and ChatGPT can still impose their own limits.

## Changes

- Delete normal/expensive global MCP throttles, principal-minute admission and credential-validation throttles rather than raising their thresholds.
- Remove obsolete rate settings, private-policy fields, validation windows and denial labels. Report exhausted monthly allowance as `principal_monthly_limit`; retain atomic usage and breaker accounting.
- Keep authorization before backend work, CSRF, credential verification, fixed capacity, deadlines, bounded retries, exact single-use basket approval and uncertain-write protections.
- Leave the product viewer, its resource identity and native ChatGPT lifecycle unchanged.
- Keep consumed credential-form CSRF tokens until expiry instead of forgetting them after 32 actions; old signed cookies cannot replay an earlier action after a burst.

## Breaking configuration change and delivery boundary

The strict private principal-policy schema rejects `principal_minute_limits` and the `minute` field in each budget window. Before a separately authorized release, remove those fields from the private policy, preserving monthly allowances, identities, credentials and revision lineage, and retain an owner-controlled rollback copy compatible with the previous code. Remove `MCP_RATE_LIMIT`, `MCP_EXPENSIVE_RATE_LIMIT`, `MCP_CREDENTIAL_RATE_LIMIT` and `MCP_CREDENTIAL_GLOBAL_RATE_LIMIT` from plain configuration; new deployment candidates do not carry them forward. Never reset usage or credential records for this transition.

Without minute throttles, authenticated bursts can saturate the fixed instance or exhaust cost budgets sooner. Credential validation is outside MCP useful-operation accounting and can generate additional login traffic. Daily/monthly operation caps and budget alerts are not a hard billing cap.

The hashed CSRF replay history grows with credential actions during their 15-minute lifetime, and expired entries are cleaned on the next action. Storage failure must fail closed before provider work, not discard still-valid tokens.

Local mock/burst checks are not production or ChatGPT acceptance. This PR neither deploys nor changes private policy/configuration, and does not establish the cause of the reported live connector interruption. External limits are not bypassed.
