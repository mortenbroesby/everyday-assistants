# Nemlig Assistant 5.0.0 - Unhurried

## In plain language

Household shopping no longer has app-local rate limits or daily/monthly quotas.
There is no normal/expensive or family/guest usage separation. Authentication and
exact basket approval remain; Nemlig and ChatGPT can still impose their own limits.

## Changes

- Delete MCP and credential-validation throttles, all usage caps/counters,
  tier budgets/reserves/shedding/forecasts and obsolete usage/reset endpoints.
- Use one strict schema-v3 family policy with an explicit owner and independent
  encrypted credentials. Reject old schemas and removed fields; no adapters or
  inline-password fallback remain.
- Preserve authorization, CSRF, account/conversation isolation, exact
  key/revision/generation binding, fixed capacity, deadlines, bounded retries,
  single-use basket approval, verification and uncertain-write protections.
- Keep consumed form CSRF hashes until expiry instead of forgetting them after
  32 actions; older signed cookies cannot replay consumed authorization.
- Preserve active reviews during credential-free profile discovery. Keep owner
  access controls available for configured members without invitation records;
  a delayed credential save cannot reactivate disabled or revoked access.
- Leave product viewer identity and native ChatGPT lifecycle unchanged.

## Breaking configuration change and delivery boundary

The release workflow also finalizes safely rolled-back failures after saving
their exact-run evidence. Pending/unknown state, cancellation, provider drift
and missing evidence still retain recovery ownership; a failed release stays
failed and cannot authorize retention.

Before a separately authorized release, privately stage schema v3 with
`revision`, `owner_subject` and `principals` entries containing only `subject`,
`principal_key` and `enabled`. Preserve exact keys and encrypted credential
revision/generation binding. Migrate any inline credential through the existing
protected onboarding with separate approval. Keep a private prior-code-compatible
rollback copy, not a runtime compatibility path.

Remove `MCP_RATE_LIMIT`, `MCP_EXPENSIVE_RATE_LIMIT`,
`MCP_CREDENTIAL_RATE_LIMIT`, `MCP_CREDENTIAL_GLOBAL_RATE_LIMIT`,
`MCP_DAILY_LIMIT` and `MCP_EXPENSIVE_DAILY_LIMIT` from deployment configuration.
Old usage records are preserved but no longer read or written. No live cleanup,
secret migration or deployment occurs in this PR.

Without count-based gates, a runaway authenticated loop can generate ongoing
provider calls, logging/storage traffic and cost until stopped. Fixed capacity,
deadlines and bounded retries constrain individual work, not aggregate usage.
The manual kill switch and advisory billing alerts are not a hard billing cap.

Hashed CSRF replay history can grow during its 15-minute validity; expired entries
are cleaned on the next action. Storage failures deny before provider work.

Local tests are not production or ChatGPT acceptance. This change neither proves
the cause of the reported live interruption nor bypasses external limits.
