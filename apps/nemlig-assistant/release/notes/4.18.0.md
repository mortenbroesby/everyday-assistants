# Nemlig Assistant 4.18.0 - Seamless

## In plain language

Your shopping review stays open while you move products between Needs review and Basket, change quantities, or compare alternatives. Basket is simpler to scan, and you can clear its local choices without touching your real Nemlig basket. A separate on-screen confirmation can submit an exact prepared review to Nemlig.

## Changes

- Keep an explicitly opened v5 review card active for matching current results; initial and historical cards remain inactive until opened.
- Preserve compatible selection and disclosure state; show Basket image, product, pack, quantity and line price first.
- Add a confirmed Clear local Basket action that moves accepted lines back to Needs review in one revision-checked local edit.
- Allow app-side confirmation through the existing protected submit tool, retaining its expiry, principal binding, freshness, single-use, uncertainty and readback safeguards. Conversation approval remains available.

## Delivery boundary

Local tests and real-MCP browser smoke do not establish native ChatGPT acceptance. Refresh the connected app and verify v5 rendering, uninterrupted local flow, and the interactive submission boundary before claiming UI delivery. Do not perform an actual Nemlig basket write solely for release smoke.
