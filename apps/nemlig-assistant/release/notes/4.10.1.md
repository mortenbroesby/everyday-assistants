# Nemlig Assistant 4.10.1 - Compass

## In plain language

This release makes account reuse and grocery matching more predictable. It avoids merging baskets during login, keeps valid sessions until they expire, treats missing reviewed products as unavailable instead of failing the whole review, and understands common Danish spellings and multipack sizes.

## Changes

- Uses the non-merging provider login contract, reuses valid sessions, and refreshes once after an unauthorized read.
- Normalizes fractional review confidence once, so `0.01` is shown as 1%.
- Keeps an exact missing product local to its proposed review while preserving authentication and cancellation failures.
- Matches `æ`, `ø`, and `å` consistently with `ae`, `oe`, and `aa` spellings.
- Calculates supported multipack totals such as `2 × 400 g` and leaves ambiguous package sizes unknown.
- Preserves read-only review behavior and the existing single-attempt mutation safeguards.
