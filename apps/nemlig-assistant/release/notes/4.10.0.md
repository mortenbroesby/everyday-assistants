# Nemlig Assistant 4.10.0 - Conductor

## In plain language

This release makes larger shopping reviews faster and easier to use. It can prepare up to fifty ingredient choices together, keeps alternatives grouped with the right product, and stops unnecessary product lookups when a review fails or is cancelled.

## Changes

- Coordinates read-only product lookups through one bounded, cancellation-aware pool while preserving result order.
- Coalesces duplicate product references and validates that resolved products match the requested identifiers.
- Supports up to fifty ingredient decisions in one picker review without artificial groups of five.
- Keeps alternatives folded inside distinct product islands and retains the approved compact mobile design.
- Consolidates picker, product-presentation, proposal-total, basket-serialization, and plan-schema code.
- Preserves sequential fresh validation and single-attempt basket mutations.
