# Nemlig Assistant 4.13.0 - Wayfinder

## In plain language

This release guides you from a shopping list through product choices and a final
review, with clear Back and Next actions before anything can be added.

## Changes

- Adds a four-step List, Proposal, optional Choices, and Approve journey.
- Lets you uncheck things you already have before Nemlig products are searched.
- Restores the actual previous step and keeps replacement choices when going back.
- Keeps product images, alternatives, match confidence, favourite labels,
  unresolved-item guidance, and expandable product details in the shared view.
- Preserves explicit approval, fresh validation, cancellation, basket readback,
  and no-retry safeguards for basket writes.
