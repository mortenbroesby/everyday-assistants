# Nemlig Assistant 4.19.0 - Lucid

## In plain language

Your shopping selection now has two clear places: In Review for product decisions and Ready for exact products you intend to send. The review stays in the same card while you select, compare, adjust quantities, and prepare an exact Nemlig basket change.

## Changes

- Keep batch acceptance as the only way to move In Review products to Ready. Replacing an alternative leaves it In Review; Ready products must move back before comparing alternatives.
- Show compact quantities and line prices, with Varebeskrivelse, Varedeklaration, and Detaljer om varen closed until opened. Disclosures do not call tools.
- Remove all Ready products from the local selection only after confirmation. Clearing the full selection shows a conversational starting screen.
- Keep the existing protected prepare, explicit confirmation, single-use submit, readback, and uncertain-write handling for Ready lines, even while other products remain In Review.
- Expose a versioned v6 viewer and use `ready` as the sole review contract, without a legacy `basket` alias. Fetched v5 resources show an inert retirement notice; already-cached documents require separate host acceptance.

## Delivery boundary

Local service, protocol, viewer and browser checks do not establish native ChatGPT acceptance. A separately approved release must refresh the connected app and verify v6 in-place interactions and safe historical-card behavior. Do not perform a real Nemlig basket write solely for release smoke.
