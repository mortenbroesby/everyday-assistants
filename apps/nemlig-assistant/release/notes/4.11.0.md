# Nemlig Assistant 4.11.0 - Lens

## In plain language

This release makes product comparisons genuinely informative. The picker now
loads the same description, declaration, and item details used by Nemlig's
product view, and it can show the rest of a normal search page instead of
stopping at two alternatives.

## Changes

- Hydrates reviewed products through Nemlig's read-only exact-product endpoint.
- Keeps descriptions, declarations, and visible item details as separate,
  folded sections and omits sections that Nemlig does not populate.
- Accepts and displays up to nine ordered alternatives for each ingredient.
- Coalesces repeated product identifiers and retains the existing maximum of
  three concurrent reads, including for a 500-reference review.
- Sanitizes and bounds provider markup and array-valued attributes before they
  reach ChatGPT or the picker.
- Preserves fresh pre-mutation validation and every existing basket safety
  boundary.
