# Nemlig Assistant 4.12.0 - Basket

## In plain language

This release lets you review the whole suggested shopping basket, correct only
the products you disagree with, and see one final recap before anything is added.

## Changes

- Shows favourite and catalogue products together in the complete proposal.
- Displays product identity, package, price, match confidence, and available
  Nemlig details in compact responsive rows.
- Presents alternatives only for challenged ingredients and keeps every other
  proposed product unchanged.
- Sends one consolidated replacement choice back to the conversation.
- Marks changed products in the final recap and hands approval to the existing
  freshly validated, single-use basket-addition flow.
- Preserves cancellation, readback, and no-retry safeguards for basket writes.
