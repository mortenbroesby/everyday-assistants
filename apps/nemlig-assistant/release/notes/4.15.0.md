# Nemlig Assistant 4.15.0 - Shortlist

## In plain language

Review products by voice or touch, keep unresolved choices separate, and build an editable local Basket. Nothing is sent to Nemlig until you review the exact products, quantities, and current prices and explicitly approve submission.

## Changes

- Compact expandable product rows, bulk acceptance, contextual alternatives, quantity editing, and explicit removal share one private temporary review.
- Needs review and local Basket remain directly accessible; alternative results survive list navigation.
- Submission refreshes exact product facts, preserves unrelated Nemlig basket lines, and retains the draft after verified success or uncertain failure without automatic retry.
- Drafts expire after one hour or a server restart. Hosts without interactive support retain conversational access to the same operations.

Checkout, ordering, payment, and delivery actions remain unavailable.
