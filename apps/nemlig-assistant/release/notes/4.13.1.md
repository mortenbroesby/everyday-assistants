# Nemlig Assistant 4.13.1 - Relay

## In plain language

This release keeps the shopping journey in one responsive view: Back and Next
work immediately, Choices is always reachable when alternatives exist, and you
can still tell ChatGPT what to add, remove, or adjust at every step.

## Changes

- Moves between the available List, Proposal, Choices, and Approve views without
  creating a separate ChatGPT response for each navigation button.
- Adds a clear Choose alternatives action to proposed products.
- Keeps normal conversation active for list edits, quantities, preferences, and
  replacements, then shows a complete updated view.
- Requires a fresh final recap whenever a conversational change is made after
  review.
- Preserves explicit approval, fresh validation, cancellation, basket readback,
  and no-retry safeguards for basket writes.
