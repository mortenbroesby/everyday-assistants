# Nemlig Assistant 6.1.4 - Lantern

## In plain language

This patch makes an interrupted production restoration resumable after the
original Worker and Container have already been restored. It validates the
restored service against the release identity recorded by that restoration,
rather than the newer recovery runner.

## Changes

- Resume a direct Container restoration after its recorded Worker restore.
- Derive the expected service version from the journal's exact starting source.
- Keep the recovery lease when the recorded source identity cannot be read or
  does not match the restored service.

## Safety

The recovery path performs no additional Container rollout or Worker rollback
once exact readback shows both were restored. Basket, Auth0, and Nemlig-provider
operations are unchanged.
